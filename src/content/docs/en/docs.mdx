Welcome to **Torto Reader** — a local-first, native cross-platform ebook reader built around Focus Mode for Windows, macOS, and Android.

Torto is dedicated to **reimagining the PC reading experience**: focusing on one meaningful reading unit at a time, Typst-inspired publication typography, keyboard-first navigation, and zero distractions. Desktop and Android share the same format-agnostic reading model and sync directly through your private cloud.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Focus Mode**: read one semantic unit at a time with a steady reading anchor; navigation, highlights, notes, footnotes and AI chat always follow the active content.
- **Native rendering**: the desktop app parses, typesets, paginates and renders in Rust without a browser; the Android app uses a pure-Dart reading core.
- **AI-assisted layout (optional)**: recognizes section headings, sourced quotations and figure or table captions, folds inline citations into markers, and recognizes text and image formulas with LaTeX copying.
- **Context-bound AI chat (optional)**: conversations attach to the active unit; type `/` for reading skills and `@` to add citations, with answers citing the source text.
- **Reading statistics**: automatically tracks reading time, history and completion, with cross-device cloud sync.
- **Formats & scanned PDF**: EPUB, MOBI, AZW/AZW3, FB2/FBZ, CBZ, CHM and PDF; scanned PDFs can be OCR'd into reflowable text.
