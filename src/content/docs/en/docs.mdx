Welcome to **Torto Reader** — a local-first, native cross-platform ebook reader built around Focus Mode for Windows, macOS, and Android.

Torto is dedicated to **reimagining the PC reading experience**: focusing on one meaningful reading unit at a time, Typst-inspired publication typography, keyboard-first navigation, and zero distractions.

---

## Configuration & Guides

- [Focus Mode Guide](../guides/focus-mode/): paragraph navigation, centered baseline anchor, sentence splitting (`3`), folded footnotes (Left `Alt`), and hidden cursor immersion.
- [OCR Providers Guide](../guides/ocr-providers/): enable full-document OCR and reflow for scanned PDFs with PaddleOCR or MinerU.
- [Cloud Storage Providers Guide](../guides/cloud-storage/): sync books, progress, highlights, and notes directly between devices using private WebDAV (with presets for InfiniCLOUD, Nutstore, and more).
